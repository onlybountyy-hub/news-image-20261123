## Hero

- **COP31 collapses in Antalya, Turkey — no deal on fossil fuels.**
  The COP31 climate conference collapsed on Sunday after 44 hours of
  overtime — the longest COP in history and the first major breakdown since
  COP15 (Copenhagen, 2009). The EU, Panama, the Marshall Islands and
  Norway rejected the "Antalya Pact" because it never mentions fossil
  fuels; COP president Murat Kurum conceded "there is no consensus". The
  US did not take part (Trump pulled out of international climate action).
  Sources: CNN (22 Nov), NPR/WCMU (updated 22 Nov 18:02 EST), phys.org /
  AFP (22 Nov), The Hindu + 6 outlets (23 Nov 01:05 IST).
